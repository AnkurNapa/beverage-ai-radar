# Beverage-AI Landscape Radar

Snapshot: 2026-07-26. 159 active, 30 dormant.

## Active companies

- **AccelPay** (multiple, order routing and marketing automation for alcohol dtc). AccelPay provides commerce infrastructure that lets alcohol brands sell direct from their own websites while staying inside the three tier system, routing each order to a licensed retailer in its network and handling age verification, payments and last mile delivery. It says it serves over 400 alcohol brands and offers native Shopify and iframe embeds. The AI content is modest: algorithmic order routing plus AI marketing automation and AI search visibility tooling. There is no evidence of substantive proprietary ML.
- **AccountTouch** (multiple, sales intelligence / depletions analytics). AccountTouch is a CRM and sales intelligence platform built specifically for beverage alcohol brands and distributors. Its AI Depletion Insights feature lets reps ask plain English questions of their depletion data instead of reading dashboards, and turns visit notes, orders and product drop-offs into a prioritised briefing per account. The AI Insights capability launched in November 2025.
- **Agricolus** (multiple, satellite vigour mapping and phytopathology forecast models for vineyards). Agricolus is an Italian precision farming platform combining satellite crop monitoring, weather station and sensor integration, scouting apps and prescription maps, with viticulture as a supported crop. Its own site cites winery customers such as Cantina Roccafiore using chlorophyll and vigour indices to find vineyard water stress, and reports 2.3 million satellite images processed across 104 countries. Honest read on the AI: the platform advertises phenology, irrigation, insect and phytopathy forecast models, which are agronomic simulation models, and it does not publicly document machine learning.
- **Agrology** (wine, disease / yield prediction). Predictive agriculture company whose in-canopy and soil sensor arrays feed machine-learning models that forecast vineyard risks up to four days ahead, including wildfire smoke taint, powdery mildew, pests and extreme weather. People: [Adam Koeppel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adamkoeppel) (Co-Founder & CEO), [Tyler Locke](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tylerlocke) (Co-Founder & CTO).
- **Ai Palette** (multiple, flavour and consumer trend prediction for drinks product development). Ai Palette runs an AI consumer-insights platform for food and drink companies, with three products: Foresight Engine for emerging trend detection, Concept Genie for AI product-concept generation and Screen Winner for virtual concept validation. Its models sit on 61 billion data points from search, social and e-retail across 24 countries and 18 languages. Diageo is a named client, using the platform to track health and wellness trends in beverage alcohol to steer R and D; it is also the vendor behind Diageo flavour-forecasting work. Offices in Singapore, Bengaluru and New York; now part of GlobalData.
- **AI4Whisky** (whiskey, carbon footprint modelling for single malt production). AI4Whisky is a free web tool from the International Centre for Brewing and Distilling at Heriot-Watt University that calculates the carbon footprint of single malt Scotch production across the distillery, maturation warehouse and bottling hall, covering Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions including raw materials, transport and waste. A published case study on a 3,000 LPA batch distillery returned 2.83 kg CO2e per 70 cl bottle, with Scope 3 accounting for 77 percent. Despite the name it is a life cycle assessment calculator, not a machine learning model. People: Shiwen Zhuang (Researcher, ICBD Heriot-Watt), Annie Hill (Professor of Brewing and Distilling).
- **Alan Talbot** (wine, data analytics / BI / market intelligence for beverage). Runs the analytics group behind the wine industry's production and market databases and data reports at WineBusiness Analytics. People: [Alan Talbot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alanltalbot/) (Director, Analytics Group (wine industry databases and data-driven analysis) — Wines Vines Analytics (WineBusiness Analytics)).
- **Alcohol Compliance Company** (multiple, compliance & licensing). AI-assisted alcohol and tobacco compliance: licensing, permitting, reporting and label approvals, with expert human review on top of the automation. Covers the regulatory layer every producer and distributor carries.
- **Algorithm Factory** (multiple, demand forecasting). Supply chain planning software provider whose Sunstice platform is tailored to breweries and beverage manufacturers. It sells AI-based demand forecasting and production optimization to beverage makers, with reference customers including Heineken, BrewDog, and Paulaner.
- **Allie AI** (multiple, factory-trained AI agents for production lines). Allie AI embeds AI agents into manufacturing operations that observe, learn, and act across machines and sensors to detect deviations and recommend corrective actions. It is used on beverage lines by producers including Heineken and PepsiCo.
- **Alpha MOS** (multiple, sensory). Alpha MOS is a publicly listed sensory-analysis solution vendor that builds electronic nose, electronic tongue, and electronic eye instruments and also runs outsourced sensory-analysis labs for food and beverage clients including beer, wine, and spirits makers. Its instruments use pattern-recognition algorithms to objectify taste and aroma.
- **Alt/Finance (ALT/FNDATA)** (multiple, wine and whisky auction price data feed). Alt/Finance is an alternative asset data provider whose AWS Data Exchange listing Real-time & Historical Data Feeds | Global Wine & Whisky Auctions carries 20-plus years of every wine and whisky lot sold at Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams and Phillips from 2000 onward, 169 fields per record, roughly 2GB, updated intraday. Wine & Spirits is one of eight asset classes they track. The product itself is a normalised data feed with no machine learning in it; the only AI angle is a Claude MCP integration that lets analysts query the verified market data from an LLM client. Delivery is via AWS Data Exchange, API, Redshift materialized views, Snowflake, FactSet and FTP.

Named customers include Diageo, Heineken, William Grant and Sons and Pabst. The GreatVines CRM is its beverage alcohol sales management product.

Founder of iWort; ships open web apps (brewing calculators, flavor-forge, brewery analytics) and writes on the gap between the number and the floor reality. In 2026 it launched AppCentral, an AI platform with AI agents and process automation for Business Central customers, and it markets predictive insights and on-demand data analysis across its food and beverage suite. The AI is agent and analytics tooling layered on ERP data rather than domain sensory or process models.
- **Aromalytics** (multiple, sensory). This is genuine supervised ML on sensor signals, not marketing framing, though it is asset reliability rather than anything about the beer recipe or fermentation itself.
