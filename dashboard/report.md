# Beverage-AI Landscape Radar

Snapshot: 2026-08-01. 240 active, 30 dormant.

## Active companies

- **ABE Beverage Equipment (American Beer Equipment)** (beer, brewhouse assisted brewing automation). ABE Beverage Equipment, formerly American Beer Equipment and a division of Norland International, builds brewhouses, fermentation, grain handling and canning lines for craft breweries. Its named control offering is the Brewhouse Automation Plus assisted brewing package, standard on new brewhouses, which automates liquor blending, vorlauf and wort grant, sparge control, lauter to kettle and whirlpool transfers, knockout to fermenter at a regulated pitch temperature, and liquor tank management with volumetric auto-stop and remote viewing and control. BrewPilot is its entry-level system for nano and brewpub scale. ABE deliberately uses off-the-shelf automation components and makes no AI or machine learning claim.
- **AccelPay** (multiple, order routing and marketing automation for alcohol dtc). AccelPay provides commerce infrastructure that lets alcohol brands sell direct from their own websites while staying inside the three tier system, routing each order to a licensed retailer in its network and handling age verification, payments and last mile delivery. It says it serves over 400 alcohol brands and offers native Shopify and iframe embeds. The AI content is modest: algorithmic order routing plus AI marketing automation and AI search visibility tooling. There is no evidence of substantive proprietary ML.
- **AccountTouch** (multiple, sales intelligence / depletions analytics). AccountTouch is a CRM and sales intelligence platform built specifically for beverage alcohol brands and distributors. Its AI Depletion Insights feature lets reps ask plain English questions of their depletion data instead of reading dashboards, and turns visit notes, orders and product drop-offs into a prioritised briefing per account. The AI Insights capability launched in November 2025.
- **Agricolus** (multiple, satellite vigour mapping and phytopathology forecast models for vineyards). Agricolus is an Italian precision farming platform combining satellite crop monitoring, weather station and sensor integration, scouting apps and prescription maps, with viticulture as a supported crop. Its own site cites winery customers such as Cantina Roccafiore using chlorophyll and vigour indices to find vineyard water stress, and reports 2.3 million satellite images processed across 104 countries. Honest read on the AI: the platform advertises phenology, irrigation, insect and phytopathy forecast models, which are agronomic simulation models, and it does not publicly document machine learning.
- **Agrology** (wine, disease / yield prediction). Predictive agriculture company whose in-canopy and soil sensor arrays feed machine-learning models that forecast vineyard risks up to four days ahead, including wildfire smoke taint, powdery mildew, pests and extreme weather. People: [Adam Koeppel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adamkoeppel) (Co-Founder & CEO), [Tyler Locke](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tylerlocke) (Co-Founder & CTO).
- **Ai Palette** (multiple, flavour and consumer trend prediction for drinks product development). Ai Palette runs an AI consumer-insights platform for food and drink companies, with three products: Foresight Engine for emerging trend detection, Concept Genie for AI product-concept generation and Screen Winner for virtual concept validation. Its models sit on 61 billion data points from search, social and e-retail across 24 countries and 18 languages. Diageo is a named client, using the platform to track health and wellness trends in beverage alcohol to steer R and D; it is also the vendor behind Diageo flavour-forecasting work. Offices in Singapore, Bengaluru and New York; now part of GlobalData.
- **AI4Whisky** (whiskey, carbon footprint modelling for single malt production). AI4Whisky is a free web tool from the International Centre for Brewing and Distilling at Heriot-Watt University that calculates the carbon footprint of single malt Scotch production across the distillery, maturation warehouse and bottling hall, covering Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions including raw materials, transport and waste. A published case study on a 3,000 LPA batch distillery returned 2.83 kg CO2e per 70 cl bottle, with Scope 3 accounting for 77 percent. Despite the name it is a life cycle assessment calculator, not a machine learning model. People: Shiwen Zhuang (Researcher, ICBD Heriot-Watt), Annie Hill (Professor of Brewing and Distilling).
- **Alan Talbot** (wine, data analytics / BI / market intelligence for beverage). Runs the analytics group behind the wine industry's production and market databases and data reports at WineBusiness Analytics. People: [Alan Talbot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alanltalbot/) (Director, Analytics Group (wine industry databases and data-driven analysis) — Wines Vines Analytics (WineBusiness Analytics)).
- **Alcohol Compliance Company** (multiple, compliance & licensing). AI-assisted alcohol and tobacco compliance: licensing, permitting, reporting and label approvals, with expert human review on top of the automation. Covers the regulatory layer every producer and distributor carries.
- **Alfa Laval** (multiple, iot condition monitoring and root cause analytics on brewery and beverage process equipment). Swedish process equipment maker supplying heat exchangers, separators, decanters, pumps and valves into breweries and beverage plants, with a hygienic processing product line covered by the brewing trade press. Its digital layer is CM Connect, a subscription condition monitor and 4G cloud gateway linking up to ten wireless vibration monitors, and Clariot, a sensor plus gateway plus analytics package that reports vibration, temperature and run time and returns root cause recommendations for process, installation and mechanical faults before breakdown. The published beverage reference is a Britvic bottling plant, and the site does not disclose the algorithms, so the analytics should not be read as a machine learning claim. In November 2025 Alfa Laval announced work on AI assisted heat exchanger service diagnostics.
- **Algorithm Factory** (multiple, demand forecasting). Supply chain planning software provider whose Sunstice platform is tailored to breweries and beverage manufacturers. It sells AI-based demand forecasting and production optimization to beverage makers, with reference customers including Heineken, BrewDog, and Paulaner.
- **Allie AI** (multiple, factory-trained AI agents for production lines). Allie AI embeds AI agents into manufacturing operations that observe, learn, and act across machines and sensors to detect deviations and recommend corrective actions. It is used on beverage lines by producers including Heineken and PepsiCo.
- **Alpha MOS** (multiple, sensory). Alpha MOS is a publicly listed sensory-analysis solution vendor that builds electronic nose, electronic tongue, and electronic eye instruments and also runs outsourced sensory-analysis labs for food and beverage clients including beer, wine, and spirits makers. Its instruments use pattern-recognition algorithms to objectify taste and aroma.
- **Alt/Finance (ALT/FNDATA)** (multiple, wine and whisky auction price data feed). Alt/Finance is an alternative asset data provider whose AWS Data Exchange listing Real-time & Historical Data Feeds | Global Wine & Whisky Auctions carries 20-plus years of every wine and whisky lot sold at Christie's, Sotheby's, Bonhams and Phillips from 2000 onward, 169 fields per record, roughly 2GB, updated intraday. Wine & Spirits is one of eight asset classes they track. The product itself is a normalised data feed with no machine learning in it; the only AI angle is a Claude MCP integration that lets analysts query the verified market data from an LLM client. Delivery is via AWS Data Exchange, API, Redshift materialized views, Snowflake, FactSet and FTP.
- **Amanda Dobbyn** (beer, data analytics / BI / market intelligence for beverage). Built a public clustering and classification analysis of 45,000-plus beers to test whether beer styles hold up statistically, a well-known beer data science project. People: [Amanda Dobbyn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amanda-dobbyn/) (Data scientist / senior data engineer; author of a widely cited beer-styles data science project — independent (beer project); now Indigo).
- **Andavi Solutions (GreatVines)** (multiple, sales execution and trade promotion analytics). Andavi Solutions sells a sales execution platform for beverage alcohol suppliers and distributors, covering CRM, trade promotion management, planogram compliance, pricing and analytics, marketed as powered by AI with an AI-powered billback management module. Named customers include Diageo, Heineken, William Grant and Sons and Pabst. The GreatVines CRM is its beverage alcohol sales management product.
- **Anheuser-Busch InBev** (beer, quality control / computer vision). AB InBev applied machine learning (Google TensorFlow, via Pluto7) to filtration sensor data at its Newark brewery, increasing barrelage per filter run by about 60 percent while stabilizing beer taste. Its Beck's brand also released Autonomous, a beer with a recipe and branding generated by AI. People: [Michel Doukeris](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michel-doukeris) (CEO).
- **Ankur Napa** (multiple, AI/ML tooling for brewing and beverage: recipe/flavor prediction, sensory analytics, consumer-review NLP, demand forecasting; ships hands-on web apps and calculators). Brewmaster-turned-data-scientist (ex-AB InBev, SABMiller, United Breweries) building AI/data tools across beer, whiskey and wine as an independent practitioner. Founder of iWort; ships open web apps (brewing calculators, flavor-forge, brewery analytics) and writes on the gap between the number and the floor reality. People: [Ankur Napa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ankur-napa/) (Independent builder — brewing data science & AI).
- **Antares Vision Group** (multiple, AI and blockchain product traceability and inspection data). Antares Vision Group provides an integrated software and hardware ecosystem for product quality inspection and end-to-end traceability. It applies artificial intelligence and blockchain to connected data management across the supply chain.
- **Anton Paar** (multiple, inline and lab beverage measurement). Measurement instruments for beverage quality control: the Alcolyzer alcohol meter, CarboQC dissolved-CO2 meter, density meters and modular packaged-beer measurement systems used inline, at-line and in the lab across beer, wine, spirits and RTDs. Over 40 years in beverage analysis. Anton Paar makes no public AI or machine-learning claim; it is tracked here as the measurement layer that brewery and winery quality data originates from, not as an AI vendor.
- **Aptean** (multiple, production data / predictive analytics). Aptean is an industry-specific ERP vendor whose Aptean Beverage ERP line, built on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations, targets breweries, distilleries, cider makers and beverage wholesale distributors with formula management, lot traceability, excise tax and bonded warehouse handling. In 2026 it launched AppCentral, an AI platform with AI agents and process automation for Business Central customers, and it markets predictive insights and on-demand data analysis across its food and beverage suite. The AI is agent and analytics tooling layered on ERP data rather than domain sensory or process models.
- **Aquacycl** (beer, remote real time control of modular wastewater reactors). Aquacycl treats ultra high strength food and beverage wastewater at source with BETT, a modular bioelectrochemical reactor using electrochemically active bacteria, avoiding dilution and cutting sludge. Every reactor is remotely monitored and controlled in real time from Aquacycl, which removes the need for a full time onsite operator, and probe data is used to read microbial colony health. Its own brewery case study documents 99 percent BOD and 85 percent TSS removal at Joshua Tree Brewery, and PepsiCo is named on the site; the company has also worked through AB InBev's 100+ Accelerator. No machine learning claim is made: this is instrumented process control, not modelling. People: Orianna Bretschger (Co-founder and CEO).
- **Aromalytics** (multiple, sensory). Aromalytics offers an AI electronic-nose platform delivered as software and hardware as a service, digitizing smell to replace human sensory panels for food and beverage quality control and adulteration detection. It serves drinks and food manufacturers plus safety agencies.
- **Arryved** (beer, taproom/point-of-sale data & analytics for craft beverage operators). Arryved is a point-of-sale and operations platform built specifically for craft beverage businesses such as breweries, taprooms, cideries and distilleries. It unifies service, online ordering, production, payments and reporting into a single data model that delivers real-time, brewery-specific analytics. Its aggregate POS dataset spans more than 2,000 taprooms and is used, with the Brewers Association, to analyze industry taproom sales trends.
- **Augury** (beer, predictive machine health on brewery packaging lines). Augury sells continuous machine-health monitoring: wireless vibration, temperature and magnetic sensors on rotating equipment feeding ML diagnostic models that classify developing faults and prescribe repairs. Heineken is named as a customer on Augury's own beverage industry page, and Augury publishes a brewery case where an alert on a filler and seamer malfunction avoided the loss of roughly 960,000 cans of beer and eight-plus hours of line downtime, plus a Top 10 global brewing company quoted on servo motor defects. This is genuine supervised ML on sensor signals, not marketing framing, though it is asset reliability rather than anything about the beer recipe or fermentation itself.
- **Authentic Vision** (multiple, quality control / computer vision). Authentic Vision sells a smartphone authentication service built on unique Holographic Fingerprint security labels that let anyone verify a bottle and detect counterfeits. Wine and spirits are core industries served, with AI-backed verification and geolocation analytics.
- **AVEVA (PI System, formerly OSIsoft)** (multiple, process data historian + predictive analytics). AVEVA sells the PI System, the operations data historian it acquired with OSIsoft in 2021, which collects, stores and contextualises real-time plant sensor data at sub-second granularity. AVEVA Predictive Analytics layers machine-learning anomaly detection on that data for early equipment-failure detection. AVEVA publishes no beer, whiskey or wine specific AI product: it is tracked here as the plant data layer that brewing and distilling analytics are built on top of, not as a beverage AI vendor.
- **Bar Analytics** (beer, beer sales and quality analytics with AI insights). Bar Analytics runs a beer analytics platform that provides real-time sales and quality data with AI insights on beer performance globally.
- **Bar.on** (beer, flavour compound modelling for on demand beer formulation). Bar.on built the One Tap, a molecular beer dispenser that constructs blond, brown, IPA, tripel and non alcoholic styles in seconds by combining flavour compounds with tap water, with alcohol level, bitterness, fruitiness, sweetness and spice adjustable per pour. The formulation work is built on the flavour compound dataset assembled over a decade by professor Kevin Verstrepen's VIB and KU Leuven lab, the same body of work behind the published Belgian beer flavour prediction models. Bar.on itself markets the dispenser and personalisation rather than a machine learning product, so treat the AI here as upstream research rather than a shipped model. Founded by CEO Dirk Standaert and CPO Valentijn Destoop, raised EUR 1.8 million seed in 2022. People: Dirk Standaert (Co-founder and CEO), Valentijn Destoop (Co-founder and CPO), Kevin Verstrepen (Research partner, VIB and KU Leuven).
- **Barrel Clarity** (whiskey, demand forecasting and computer vision data capture). Barrel Clarity is distillery management software for craft spirits producers covering barrel inventory, production operations and one-click TTB report filing. It advertises two AI features: forecasting that analyses a distillery's own sales history to predict future demand, and computer vision data entry where a phone photo of a gauge or hydrometer is read and logged automatically. The product is pre-launch, marketed as launching soon behind a founding member waitlist, so the AI is a stated capability rather than an observed shipping one. No funding, founders or location are disclosed on the site.
- **Bart Watson** (beer, data analytics / BI / market intelligence for beverage). PhD economist who built the Brewers Association's craft beer production, sales, and market intelligence data program and was long the public face of US beer industry statistics. People: [Bart Watson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bart-watson/) (President and CEO, former Chief Economist (craft beer market data and analytics) — Brewers Association).
- **BeerBoard** (multiple, demand forecasting). On-premise alcohol management company whose Alcohol Intelligence platform captures and analyzes real-time draft and inventory data across beer, wine and spirits for bars and restaurant chains. People: [Mark Young](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mark-young-7b8b554) (Founder and CEO).
- **Beerhive** (beer, sensory data mining and beer recommendation). Beerhive, operated by Belgian company imkr BV in Leuven, runs a community beer tasting platform that scores beers against about 125 sensory flavour parameters rather than style categories, with roughly 10,600 beers and 2,170 breweries catalogued. It sells the resulting sensory data back to brewers as consumer perception insight, and offers venues a personalised digital beer advisor. The company describes itself as sensory data mining and offers preference based recommendations, but publishes no explicit machine learning claim and does not describe the underlying method. Founded by zythologists Jan Paesen and Wim De Bruycker and part of the Start it @KBC accelerator. People: Jan Paesen (Founder), Wim De Bruycker (Co-founder).
- **BEERSOFT** (beer, brewery customer service and operations agents). BEERSOFT builds custom AI and agent implementations specifically for breweries, taprooms, brewpubs and beverage brands across the US and Canada. Products include RAG systems grounded in brewery documents such as menus and policies, customer service chatbots answering taproom hours, beer availability, events and reservations, and autonomous agents that process reservations and generate reports. It integrates with brewery systems including Square, Toast and Ekos. AWS Bedrock is one of the named deployment platforms alongside OpenAI API, LangChain and Azure AI, chosen for VPC integration and enterprise security. Offices listed in Fredericton, Saint John and North Vancouver.
- **beON consult GmbH** (beer, IT, data, and AI consultancy for breweries). beON consult is an IT consultancy focused on IT, data, and AI for breweries and brewery groups. It helps brewers connect and evolve digital brewing processes across the full IT lifecycle.
- **Berkeley Yeast** (multiple, synthetic biology strain engineering for flavour, no machine learning claim). Berkeley Yeast is a UC Berkeley spinout that uses synthetic biology to engineer brewing and wine yeast strains, selling FRESH low diacetyl strains, TROPICS and SUPERBLOOM thiol releasing strains for hop-like aroma, SUMMERSTONE, the GALACTIC souring strain and non-alcoholic beer strains in dry and liquid formats. It was co-founded by Charles Denby, Nick Harris and Rachel Li with UC Berkeley professor Jay Keasling as key inventor, and is funded by NSF, NIH and USDA grants plus venture capital. Its own website makes no machine learning claim: the AI angle in press coverage belongs to a separate Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory project using the ART Bayesian recommendation tool to tune hop-flavour metabolite production, which does not name the company. Included as the flagship engineered-strain supplier on the ingredient layer, with the AI claim explicitly not attributed to it. People: Charles Denby (Co-founder), Nick Harris (Co-founder), Rachel Li (Co-founder).
- **BinaryBeer** (beer, smart keg supply chain insights). BinaryBeer builds KegLink, a low power IoT device that turns a keg into a tracked container reporting location, temperature and beer freshness, plus the Binary Cloud platform that brewers use for fleet maps, alerts and reporting. The company states the platform is "powered by machine learning and AI-driven algorithms" that "continuously refine its insights" for fleet activity and supply chain optimisation, though it does not name the models or specific predictions. Asahi Europe and International selected BinaryBeer for an international smart keg deployment, and the product is also sold in North America through a partnership with Kegshoe. People: [Michael Burton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-burton/) (Founder and CEO).
- **BitMetrics** (multiple, computer vision quality inspection of wine during bottling). BitMetrics is a Barcelona deep-tech company building vision-guided robotics and AI visual inspection software (Pick+ and Scan+) for industrial lines. Its wine application is the VINIA project with Familia Torres, where image sensors and machine learning models spin each bottle to detect and isolate foreign particles in the wine during bottling, piloted at the Torres winery in Pacs del Penedes. The project received about 29,000 EUR of ACCIO innovation funding. Wine is one vertical inside a broader industrial automation business. People: Mar Masulli (CEO).
- **BrauKon** (beer, brewhouse process automation and recipe control). BrauKon is a Bavarian brewhouse and brewery plant OEM serving craft and mid-size breweries. It ships two named control products: BrauControl, a full brewery process automation system covering malt intake through to bright beer tank and CIP, with 60 recipe positions of 30 steps each, weekly brew scheduling, a brewing log and remote maintenance over a network connection, built almost entirely on Siemens components; and BrauKon TControl for brewhouse steps such as mashing, lautering and wort boiling, also available with remote maintenance. BrauControl includes self-optimising fuzzy logic lautering control that adjusts rake height, pump speed and sparging, which is classical control engineering and not machine learning. BrauKon makes no AI claim.
- **Brewers Association (Barley assistant)** (beer, retrieval assistant over brewing technical and regulatory resources). The Brewers Association, the US craft brewing trade body, launched Barley in February 2026, a chat assistant that answers member questions about brewing techniques, industry data, legislative updates and regulatory guidance. It is deliberately constrained to Brewers Association content rather than the open web, and improves through thumbs up and down feedback from members. Technically this is a retrieval assistant over a curated corpus, not novel brewing ML, but it is a notable signal of institutional AI adoption in craft beer.
- **Brewing-AI** (beer, recipe / flavor prediction). Brewing-AI, a solution from AlpVision SA, offers breweries a deep-reinforcement-learning system that combines operator settings with near-infrared spectroscopy and sensor data to recommend optimal fermentation parameters and hold beer quality steady across batches. It is sold as a process-optimization service to brewers.
- **BrewPlanner** (multiple, production scheduling and batch planning for craft beverage producers). BrewPlanner is production planning and light ERP software for craft breweries, wineries, distilleries, cideries and meaderies, covering drag and drop batch scheduling, recipe management, raw material tracking and production analytics. It claims over 500 brewery and winery customers, from nano scale to multi-site regional operations. The site makes no artificial intelligence or machine learning claim: the scheduling is deterministic planning, and the quoted results such as 99 percent on-time scheduling and 30 percent waste reduction are customer outcomes rather than model outputs.
- **Briggs of Burton** (multiple, no ai claim; process engineering design and build for breweries and distilleries). Process engineering contractor tracing its history to 1732, now part of the CLPT Group, designing brewhouses, cold block equipment, stills, ageing and blending processes for brewers and distillers. Named work includes Scotch whisky producers and a Mexican tequila plant running 140,000 litres per day, and it markets a combined BrewStillery technology. Services run from feasibility study through project management and commissioning. Its own site makes no artificial intelligence, machine learning or data analytics claim; it is included as an engineering layer that shapes what beverage plant data exists at all.
- **Buhler Group** (multiple, condition-based predictive maintenance and yield analytics via the buhler insights iiot platform). Swiss process technology group supplying malting plants, grain handling and milling for brewing and distilling, and stating on its own site that 75 percent of the world's malt is processed on its machines. Its brewing and distilling pages name Octopi Brewing in Waunakee as a customer, and its Minch Malt story describes Buhler Insights as the digital backbone of a new Irish malting plant with batch-by-batch dashboards. Buhler Insights is described as condition-based predictive maintenance, downtime and yield analytics; Buhler does not claim machine learning for it, and the named Insights references on its site are flour milling rather than brewing sites.
- **Cambrian Innovation** (multiple, remote monitoring of brewery wastewater treatment). Cambrian Innovation is an MIT spinout that treats brewery and winery effluent on site with bioelectrically enhanced anaerobic systems, EcoVolt and BlueCycle MBR, recovering biogas and reuse water, and sells it as a Water Energy Purchase Agreement so the producer puts up no capital. Its own site names Anheuser Busch Houston, Russian River Brewing, Bear Republic Brewing, Northern United Brewing and Rombauer Vineyards as customers, and Lagunitas in Petaluma has run an EcoVolt system since 2014 under a ten year operations agreement. The digital layer is cloud based real time monitoring and analytics of reactor operation; the company makes no machine learning claim. Relevant to the water stewardship and effluent side of beverage ESG. People: Bobb Campbell (CEO).
- **CannyCask** (whiskey, cask ownership verification). CannyCask runs a whisky cask trading platform that verifies ownership of casks held in HMRC-regulated Scottish bonded warehouses. Its DAV VERIFIED mechanism cross-checks a claimed cask against warehouse custodial records and writes a dual-signed provenance record, with Polygon for the ledger, BitGo for custody, Onfido for identity checks and IPFS for document storage. At time of reading the site reported 22 verified casks worth over 99,000 pounds across two warehouses. There is no AI or machine learning in the product; it is cryptographic record keeping aimed at cask investment fraud.
- **CAP Vision (CAP Vignes)** (wine, production data). CAP Vision is a French Microsoft Dynamics partner whose CAP Vignes app is published on Microsoft AppSource for Dynamics 365 Business Central. It serves winemakers, wine estates, cooperatives, wine merchants and distilleries with vinification and production management, stock tracking, DRM and customs declarations, and sales. Named customers include Bestheim, Hugel et Fils and Ethic Drinks. No public AI or machine learning claims; tracked as a wine and spirits system of record.
- **CARL GmbH** (whiskey, distilling process control and automatic fractionation). CARL GmbH, the Christian Carl still works founded in 1869, builds pot stills and distillation plants for craft and commercial distilleries worldwide. Its named control products are DPC 510 (Distilling Process Control), a 20 inch touchscreen system regulating heating and cooling, temperature monitoring, safety interlocks and automatic fractionation with recipe management; the compact DPC 200/210 TOUCH with optional remote access for retrofitting older CARL stills; CADi (Computer Aided Distilling) with integrated flow and alcohol measurement for fully automatic runs; and DPC 50, a calculator that works out dilution water and duty. CARL makes no AI or machine learning claim; this is recipe-driven process control and the origin point of distillery process data.

CaskX sells bourbon barrel and Scotch cask ownership to private investors and runs periodic valuations of client holdings. Offices in Los Angeles, Louisville, London, Toronto, Sydney and Hong Kong. There is no machine learning here, and no independently audited index; it is a broker with an internal valuation dataset, which is worth stating plainly given how often cask investment marketing implies more.

Co-founded Enolytics in 2016 to bring big-data business intelligence to wine and spirits companies worldwide, building analytics apps on the Qlik platform. People: [Cathy Huyghe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cathy-huyghe-332307/) (Co-Founder and CEO, wine and spirits data analytics / business intelligence — Enolytics).
- **Cellr** (wine, serialised bottle track and trace). Kubota Corporation invested in the company in 2023.

International producer and marketer of beer, wine and spirits with operations in the US, Mexico, New Zealand and Italy, including Corona, Modelo, Robert Mondavi Winery and High West Whiskey.
