# Beverage-AI Landscape Radar

Snapshot: 2026-07-21. 7 active, 10 dormant.

## Active companies

- **BeerBoard** (multiple, demand forecasting). On-premise alcohol management company whose Alcohol Intelligence platform captures and analyzes real-time draft and inventory data across beer, wine and spirits for bars and restaurant chains.
- **Crafted ERP** (multiple, demand forecasting). Beverage ERP built on Oracle NetSuite by Doozy Solutions, offering breweries and distilleries AI-driven demand forecasting, inventory optimization and production planning across beer and spirits.
- **Deep Liquid** (beer, recipe / flavor prediction). Adelaide company using generative AI and machine learning to design personalized beverage recipes, starting in craft beer with the award-winning Rodney AI IPA and later expanding into wellness drinks.
- **Deep Planet** (wine, quality control / computer vision). Founded by Oxford researchers, its VineSignal platform combines satellite imagery, ground sensors and machine learning to predict grape maturity, yield, soil moisture and disease pressure for vineyards.
- **Enolytics** (wine, demand forecasting). Data analytics platform built for wineries and spirits brands that aggregates sales channel data and applies predictive analytics and forecasting to drive direct-to-consumer growth.
- **Precision Fermentation (Sennos)** (beer, quality control / computer vision). Maker of the BrewMonitor real-time fermentation monitoring system for breweries, using IoT sensors and adaptive analytics trained on tens of thousands of fermentations to flag events and anomalies. Rebranded to Sennos.
- **Sommelier.bot** (multiple, sensory). Berlin-based AI agent for the wine and spirits industry offering a chatbot that gives consumers personalized drink recommendations.
